Creamy Chicken and Mushroom Pasta

A comforting and creamy pasta dish made with tender chicken, mushrooms and Parmesan cheese. Perfect for a quick weeknight meal and ready in around 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 300g Pasta (penne or fettuccine)
  • 2 chicken breasts, washed with lemon, and diced
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 300ml double cream
  • 50g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp mixed herbs or Italian seasoning
  • Fresh parsley, chopped (optional, for garnish)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta according to packet instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat the olive oil and butter in a large frying pan over a medium heat.
  3. Add the diced chicken, season with salt, pepper and herbs, then cook for 6-8 minutes until golden and fully cooked.
  4. Stir in the mushrooms and garlic, cooking for 3-4 minutes until the mushrooms have softened.
  5. Pour in the double cream and bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Add the Parmesan cheese and stir until the sauce is smooth and creamy.
  7. Add the cooked pasta to the pan and toss until evenly coated in the sauce.
  8. Garnish with fresh parsley, if desired, and serve immediately.

Serving Suggestion: Serve with garlic bread and a fresh green salad for a complete meal.
